Protection

Ceramic Coating

Full-vehicle ceramic packages for lasting shine and protection.

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied by hand that chemically bonds to your paint, forming a hard, glass-like layer that lasts years — not weeks like a wax. It's the single best thing you can do for your paint: a deeper, wetter-looking gloss, dirt and water that just slide right off so washes take half the time, and real protection against UV fading, oxidation, and the minor scratches that quietly kill resale value. If you want your car looking showroom-new every time someone sees it, this is the upgrade that actually pays for itself.

Please note: ceramic coating isn’t a mobile service — it needs a controlled space to cure properly, so you’ll drop your vehicle off with us and we’ll need it for about 2 days.
